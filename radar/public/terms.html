Terms of use

The deal, in plain words.

Using Radar means accepting these terms. The most important one is the third: Radar is not the organisation running anything it lists.

Last updated 22 September 2026

1. What Radar is

Radar is a directory of opportunities for students, a matching tool and a personal application tracker. It is free to use. Some features need an account.

2. Your account

Give an email address you control and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account. If you are under 16, get permission from a parent, guardian or teacher first. You can delete your account at any time from account settings.

3. Radar does not run these opportunities

Every competition, programme, scholarship and fellowship listed belongs to the organisation running it. That organisation sets the deadlines, the eligibility rules, the required documents and the outcome. Radar has no influence over any of it.

Specifically:

  • Radar does not guarantee eligibility. A match score describes how the facts you entered line up with the facts on the listing. It is not a ruling on whether you may apply.
  • Radar does not guarantee acceptance. Nothing here improves your odds by itself.
  • Radar does not guarantee that a deadline is current. Dates are read from official pages and carry the date they were read. Organisers change dates without notice. Always confirm on the official page before relying on one.
  • Radar does not control application links. If an organiser's page moves or closes, the link may fail before Radar notices.

Check the official source before you apply, and treat what Radar shows as a starting point rather than an authority.

4. Accuracy and reports

Radar works to keep listings correct and shows the date each was last checked, including when it has not been checked yet. Mistakes still happen. If you find one, report it — reports are reviewed and corrections are made.

5. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to break, overload or gain unauthorised access to Radar; do not scrape the catalogue in bulk to republish it; do not use Radar to send anything unlawful, abusive or misleading, including false reports about listings. Accounts that do these things can be closed.

6. Your content

Your notes, checklist items and profile answers stay yours. Radar stores them to run the product for you and does not publish them, sell them or show them to other users.

7. Availability

Radar is provided as it is. It may be unavailable for maintenance or for reasons outside its control. Features described as not yet built may change or may never ship.

8. Liability

To the extent the law allows, Radar is not liable for a missed deadline, a rejected application, an ineligible application or any other loss arising from relying on information here rather than on the organiser's own page. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

9. Ending it

You can stop using Radar and delete your account whenever you like. Radar may close an account that breaks these terms, and will say why where it can.

10. Changes

These terms may change. The date above changes with them, and material changes are communicated to account holders. Continuing to use Radar after a change means accepting the new version.

11. Contact

Questions about these terms go through the contact form.
