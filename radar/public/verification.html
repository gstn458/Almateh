Verification policy

What the badge on a listing actually means.

Radar would rather say "we have not checked this yet" than show a confident date that is wrong. Here is what each state means and how a listing moves between them.

Verified

A person read the official page

Someone opened the organiser's own page, confirmed the deadline, eligibility and funding, and recorded the date. The listing shows that date.

Needs review

The source is known, the details are not confirmed

Radar has the organisation and the official URL but has not re-read the page this cycle. No deadline is asserted as fact and the listing says so on its face.

Expired

The cycle has closed

The published deadline has passed. Recurring programmes stay listed so you can plan for the next round, marked closed rather than quietly deleted.

The checks

Source check

A listing must trace back to the organisation running the opportunity. Aggregators are listed only as what they are — directories — and labelled that way.

Deadline confirmation

Dates are read off the official page and stored with the form the organiser used. Where a programme has no fixed public date, the listing carries variable or rolling rather than a guess. A date that depends on a regional fair, a campus nomination or a national committee is marked as such, because that local deadline is the one that will actually catch you out.

Link testing

Application links are fetched in a check run and the response recorded. A link that fails is flagged for review and the listing is not treated as verified until a person has looked at it.

Re-checking

A listing older than 90 days is shown as due a re-check, so an old date can never sit on the page looking fresh.

What is not automated yet

Radar does not continuously watch organiser pages for changes. Verification runs on a schedule and on reports. Until that changes, nothing in the interface will claim otherwise.

When Radar gets it wrong

Tell it. Every listing has a report link, and the report form takes a deadline change, a broken link, a closed programme or a wrong eligibility rule. Reports are reviewed, and a change to a listing records who made it and when.
