Deadlines

People rarely miss things because they never heard about them.

They heard in August and the form closed in December. Radar puts every date you save on one calendar, sets your reminders, and exports to the calendar you already use.

Verification

A badge that means something.

Listings rot. Deadlines move, programmes quietly close, dead links sit online for years. Radar shows the date a listing was last read by a person — and says plainly when that has not happened yet.

Source checked

The listing traces back to the organisation running it, not to an aggregator that copied it.

Deadline read from the source

Dates come off the official page. Where the organiser has not published one, Radar says so instead of inventing it.

Link tested

Application links are fetched on a check run. When one breaks, the listing is flagged for review.
